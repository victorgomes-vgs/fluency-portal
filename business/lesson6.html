In Practice
Thesis Statement
The thesis statement must be a strong sentence that reveals the main idea of the speech. Using personal experiences is sometimes a good way to show your audience that you are speaking from true knowledge and not just creating a hypothesis about something. Look at the example below, based on the speech by Steve Jobs.
"Out of all the many aspects of my life and personality that have helped me achieve success in all areas, there is one that has, above all, determined whether my success would be long lasting."
Would you consider that a good thesis statement?
Debate
