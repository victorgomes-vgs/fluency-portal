English Language

Everyone says you should learn a new language and when people talk about it, the first language that crosses our minds is the English language. But why is it so important? Well, firstly, it's spoken by more than 380 million native speakers from countries such as the United States, the U.K., Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, India, South Africa, Nigeria and many Caribbean islands such as Barbados and Trinidad and Tobago. Moreover, because of the economic importance of most of these countries and the global influence of cinema and music, English is also the most widely learned second language in the world. This means that by learning English you'll be able to travel around the entire world and you'll be understood by most people even if you're not visiting an English-speaking country. Isn't that awesome? Besides traveling, English can certainly open innumerable doors in most professional fields. More than half of the world's technical and scientific periodicals are written in English, and three quarters of all emails worldwide are sent in English, too. So, are you ready to talk to the world?

"Besides traveling, English can certainly open innumerable doors in most professional fields."
