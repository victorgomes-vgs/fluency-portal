Team Player or Lone Wolf?

Although working in groups is nice and often quite beneficial, every once in a while you will meet someone who has little interest in working with his or her peers. Sometimes people prefer solitude rather than constant association with others.

Some people would rather spend time alone to think or relax. They may use this alone time for reflection and meditation. Some people thrive on their privacy and quiet time. Group work often becomes a burden to those who prefer to work alone. Depending on the person, work can be accomplished faster and better when working by themselves.

Is it healthy to always be alone? Is it healthy to always be in constant association with other people? The most probable answer is no, to both questions. Balance in all things brings happiness and success.

Working alone, relaxing and meditating allows us to reflect and learn from ourselves. But, when we work with others we learn how to deal successfully with many different people; besides the fact that being able to collaborate with others is a requirement in mostly all workplaces, where one will need to be able to share ideas and accept criticism.
