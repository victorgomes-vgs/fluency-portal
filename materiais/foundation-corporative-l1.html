The economy is stable following the problems of the past two years. By following a tight monetary policy, the government has reduced the inflation rate to two per cent. For borrowers, after going up dramatically, the interest rate is now down to eight per cent. The last six months have seen a slight improvement in the exchange rate against the dollar. For the country as a whole, the GDP has grown by point one five per cent. Exports are increasing, and the balance of trade is starting to look much healthier. In terms of jobs, the unemployment rate continues to be a problem, as it is still 16 per cent. In order to stimulate the economy and attract foreign investment from abroad, the government is offering new tax incentives, as well as making a renewed effort to reduce government bureaucracy and red tape. Finally, a large skilled labour force means there could be attractive investment opportunities over the next five years.
