3. Grammar — Simple Present, 3ª Pessoa do Singular

Na terceira pessoa do singular (he / she / it), o verbo principal recebe a terminação -s (ou -es) na forma afirmativa. As formas negativa e interrogativa utilizam o auxiliar does, e o verbo principal retorna à forma base (sem -s).
FormaEstruturaExemplo
AfirmativaSujeito + verbo(-s/-es) + complementoShe works at the office.
NegativaSujeito + does not (doesn't) + verbo (base) + complementoShe doesn't work on weekends.
InterrogativaDoes + sujeito + verbo (base) + complemento?Does she work every day?

10 Frases — He / She / It

  1. He — afirmativa He drinks coffee every morning.
  2. She — negativa She doesn't eat meat for dinner.
  3. It — interrogativa Does it need water every day?
  4. He — negativa He doesn't speak with his boss on weekends.
  5. She — afirmativa She studies English at school in the morning.
  6. It — afirmativa It opens early and closes late.
  7. He — interrogativa Does he understand the task for tomorrow?
  8. She — interrogativa Does she like tea or coffee for breakfast?
  9. It — negativa It doesn't close in the afternoon.
  10. He — afirmativa He lives downtown and works with his friend every day.
