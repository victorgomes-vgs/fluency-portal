LESSON 8
Languages & Countries

I need to visit my family

Sua família mora no exterior e você quer visitá-los. Você precisa pedir para o seu chefe.

Learning Objectives

  • Can name some members of the family and some acquaintances.
  • Can name a few countries.
  • Can say some places they go to.
  • Can say what they do at some places.

Grammar

  • Subject pronouns we and they in the interrogative form;
  • Preposition of direction to;
