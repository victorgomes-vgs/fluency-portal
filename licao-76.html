Learning a New Language

Maria wants to speak English fluently. She studies every day. She practices her reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills at home.

Maria has a good ability to learn new words. She takes tests every week and improves her pronunciation a lot. Communication is not easy, but she doesn't give up.

At school, her teacher speaks clearly. Sometimes Maria doesn't get it. She says: "Sorry, I can't hear you. Can you repeat, please?"

Maria can write very well now. She can also talk to people in public. Her friend says: "You can improve your writing by reading — and you really do!"

Maria wants to go on an exchange program. She needs to fill out an application form. It isn't simple, but she can do it.

Every day, she takes a short break and studies again. Little by little, she speaks better and better.
