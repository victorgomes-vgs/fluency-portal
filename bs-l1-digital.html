Lesson 01

Business English
Problema
You were laid off 3 months ago. What had already happened before that you didn’t notice — and what would you have done differently?
Past Perfect  /  Third Conditional  /  First Conditional
Objetivo
Refletir sobre uma demissão, analisando causas anteriores e decisões futuras.
Language Function
Talking about what has / had happened before a past event, and imagining how things could have turned out differently.
